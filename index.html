2026 · Journal article Journal of Environmental Management

Dent corn yield and grain protein responses to integrated composted sewage sludge and liquid centrate fertilization compared with chemical fertilizer

Jean Claude Tuyisenge, Luc Duc Phung, Hiroki Matsuyama, Shuji Urakawa, and Toru Watanabe

a Iwate University · Japan · b Rwanda Polytechnic, Huye College, Department of Agricultural Engineering · Huye District, Rwanda · c Yamagata University · Japan · d Japan Society for the Promotion of Science (JSPS) · Japan

Abstract

This study evaluated composted sewage sludge (CSS) as a basal fertilizer and liquid centrate (LC) as a topdressing nutrient source for dent corn, as alternatives or complements to chemical NPK fertilizer. In a field experiment with six fertilizer treatments, LC at the highest tested rate increased grain protein, shoot nitrogen uptake, and stover yield. Compared with NPK alone, CSS combined with the highest LC rate increased grain protein by 9.4%, nitrogen uptake by 35.3%, and stover yield by 24.7%. Grain yield (9.9–11.2 t ha−1) and plant height were not affected. The findings highlight the potential to recycle wastewater-derived nutrients under the conditions tested.
